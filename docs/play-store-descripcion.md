### Full description (max 4000 characters)

CamRun is the official app of CAM (Centro de Apoyo a la Mujer), a non-profit that supports women in vulnerable situations. Train, record your runs and sign up for CAM races: every race you run supports that work.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN THAT ADAPTS
Pick the race you are chasing and CamRun lays out the weeks for you. See each day's session on the calendar, move workouts when life gets in the way, and keep the plan pointed at your goal. Every session shows distance, type and target pace.

TRACK EVERY RUN LIVE
GPS draws your route as you go. Distance, current and average pace, elapsed time and per-kilometre splits stay on screen. Set a distance or time goal before you start, pause and resume whenever you need, and save the run with its map and full summary when you are done.

YOUR COMPLETE HISTORY

ALL IN ONE PLACE

Run for yourself. Run for CAM.

## Antes de pegarlo

- Play prohíbe emojis, MAYÚSCULAS excesivas en el título, y frases tipo "la #1"
  o "descarga ya" en la descripción. Los encabezados en mayúsculas de arriba son
  aceptables pero si el revisor objeta, pásalos a mayúscula inicial.
- El texto describe el plan de entrenamiento, el historial y el kilometraje de
  zapatillas: si alguna de esas pantallas sigue con datos locales al publicar,
  bórrala del texto antes de enviar. Google rechaza descripciones que prometen
  funciones que la app no cumple.
- Los dos bloques mencionan ubicación precisa, que es lo que Play espera ver
  declarado también en el formulario de permisos sensibles (§5 de
  `play-store-publicacion.md`).
